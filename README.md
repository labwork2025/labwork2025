## My Journey: From Mission-Driven Service to AI Governance

My career has been defined by two core principles forged during my U.S. Army service: a **protective, selfless mindset** and a deep-seated discipline for **managing complex systems** (honed as both an Infantryman and a Supply Specialist).

I translated this blend of mission and method from the military to a hands-on technical career as an HVAC Service Technician, where I diagnosed and solved complex systems problems.

In 2024, I pivoted this entire skill set from the physical to the digital world, driven by a passion to secure the technologies we rely on.

I built my foundation methodically (Google Cybersecurity Certificate, TryHackMe SOC Analyst path) and then moved immediately into practice by building my own multi-VM lab (Security Onion, Metasploitable2) to analyze real-world threats.

While building my SOC skills, I saw the next great challenge emerging: the explosive growth of artificial intelligence. It was here my **protective mindset** truly clicked with my new technical goals. I realized that my interest wasn't just in *reacting* to threats. I wanted to be part of the solution, **proactively contributing to the frameworks** that enable the secure and responsible use of new technology. My goal is to help build that trust so society can benefit from AI safely.

This insight prompted my current specialization in AI Governance. I am now building an advanced portfolio (NIST AI RMF assessments, T-Pot honeypot) and pursuing the IAPP AIGP certification (target March 2026) to apply my unique background to this critical mission.
